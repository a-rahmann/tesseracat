## 1. THE STORY & LORE

### 🌌 The Incident at Compound 666
In the remote Martian moon base of **Phobos**, deep beneath the red crust inside **Facility Sector 666**, the United Aerospace Research Division was testing quantum subspace gate technology. During a high-voltage dimensional teleportation sequence, containment catastrophically ruptured. 

Instead of opening a transit corridor to Earth, the rift tore through the demonic underworld. Hordes of flesh-eating crawlers, fire-infused imps, and armored cybernetic brutes spilled into the corridors, massacring military security and desecrating the installation.

### 🪖 Your Mission: The Lone Survivor
You are the **last surviving combat Marine** of the Vanguard Recon unit. All communications and network satellites are completely dark. You have **zero outside support**, no uplink to headquarters, and zero internet connectivity.

Your objective is simple:
> **Fight your way through the infested compound, unlock the security sector airlocks, eliminate the gatekeeper demons, activate the dimensional extraction teleporter, and escape back home.**

## 2. WHAT TO DO: MISSION WALKTHROUGH

The facility is laid out as a moderately expanded **64x64 sector grid** (4,096 tiles), engineered specifically for tight pacing (~3-5 minutes per sector), multi-lane combat, flanking loops, and rewarding secrets. 

You must navigate the three interconnected sectors, acquire security keys, defeat the Cyber-Demon bosses, and unseal the evacuation airlock:

```
[SECTOR 1: HANGAR] ➔ Acquire BLUE KEYCARD (59.5, 4.5) ➔ Unlock BLUE SECURITY DOOR (X:16, Y:19)
         ↓
[SECTOR 2: PLAZA]  ➔ Acquire RED SKULL KEY (59.5, 37.5) ➔ Unseal RED DEMONIC GATE (X:16, Y:41)
         ↓
[SECTOR 3: ARENA]  ➔ Slay Colosseum Boss Swarm ➔ Step into EVACUATION AIRLOCK (X:59.5, Y:59.5)
```

### Phase 1: Sector 1 — Grand Hangar & Blue Keycard (Rows 0 – 19)
* **Spawn Coordinates:** `(X: 3.5, Y: 3.5)` on the West Landing Pad.
* **Objective:** Find the **Blue Security Keycard** and open the **Blue Security Door**.

1. **Equip and Arm:** You spawn armed with a **Tactical Combat Knife** and an **AK-47 Assault Rifle** loaded with 25 rounds. Grab initial health and armor at `(2.5 - 5.5, 2.5)`.
2. **Room Shapes & Tactical Cover:**
   - **Landing Pad (X: 1..13, Y: 1..10):** Features concrete pillars at `(4, 5)` and `(9, 5)` to dodge initial Crawler attacks.
   - **South Barracks (X: 1..13, Y: 12..18):** Tight interconnected living quarters with door corners ideal for close-range shotgun blasts.
   - **Loop / Shortcut 1:** A south corridor links the Barracks directly into the Central Concourse `(14, 14)`, allowing you to flank roaming demons without backtracking.
   - **Secret Room 1 (X: 1, Y: 16):** Press **`[E]`** on the west barracks wall at `(1, 16)` to slide open a concealed pushwall hiding an early **Super Shotgun** and shell box!
3. **Central Hangar Concourse (X: 15..45, Y: 1..18):** 
   - A wide open hall with 4 massive support pillars at `(22, 5)`, `(36, 5)`, `(22, 12)`, and `(36, 12)`. Use these pillars for cover while picking off Imps with the AK-47.
   - **Secret Room 2 (X: 29, Y: 2):** Press **`[E]`** on the north storage wall at `(29, 3)` to reveal a hidden **200% Supercharge Mega-Health** sphere!
4. **East Vault Complex (X: 46..62, Y: 1..18):**
   - Winding S-bend security chicanes that prevent demons from sniping you across the map.
   - **Loop / Shortcut 2:** A secondary maintenance corridor at `(45, 14)` connects the Vault back to the Central Hangar.
5. **Acquire Blue Keycard:** Located in the reinforced Northeast Vault at **`(X: 59.5, Y: 4.5)`** guarded by an Imp slasher pack.
6. **Open the Blue Door:** Follow the green guide arrows (`>>>`) south to coordinate **`(X: 16, Y: 19)`** and press **`[E]`** to unlock Sector 2!

### Phase 2: Sector 2 — Central Tech Plaza & Red Skull Key (Rows 20 – 41)
* **Starting Coordinates:** Rows 20 through 41 of the compound.
* **Objective:** Find the **Red Demonic Skull Key** and unseal the **Red Demonic Gate**.

1. **Room Shapes & Multi-Lane Exploration:**
   - **Central Server Plaza (X: 14..48, Y: 22..40):** A tech center featuring 4 computer terminal islands (Tile 3) providing crouching/strafing cover blocks.
   - **Dual Bridges across Toxic Canal:** A toxic sludge moat (Tile 4) divides the plaza. Two bridges (West at `X: 25`, East at `X: 37`) offer tactical choices to cross or retreat.
   - **Loop / Shortcut 3:** A catwalk at `(13, 35)` allows swift transit between the West Drainage labs and the Central Plaza.
2. **Secret Weapon Lab & Plasma Carbine:**
   - **Secret Room 3 (X: 1, Y: 38):** Pushwall hiding combat armor and plasma cells.
   - **Secret Room 4 (X: 31, Y: 22):** Press **`[E]`** on the computer server partition at `(31, 22)` to uncover the **Plasma Carbine** at `(31.5, 23.5)`!
3. **Blood Catacombs & First Cyber-Brute Boss:**
   - Advance east into the obsidian rune crypts (X: 50..62).
   - Guarding the Red Skull Key is an elite **Cyber-Brute Boss** (200 HP) at `(58.5, 34.5)`.
   - **Tactics:** Use the crypt pillars at `(52, 33)` and `(52, 37)` to break line of sight, and shoot the explosive barrels at `(55.5, 32.5)` to shred the boss!
4. **Grab the Red Key:** Step over the crimson skull key at **`(X: 59.5, Y: 37.5)`**.
5. **Unseal the Red Gate:** Walk to the dividing gate at **`(X: 16, Y: 41)`** and press **`[E]`** to enter Sector 3.

### Phase 3: Sector 3 — The Abyssal Colosseum & Evacuation Airlock (Rows 42 – 63)
* **Starting Coordinates:** Rows 42 through 63 of the compound.
* **Objective:** Traverse the colosseum, neutralize the boss horde, and escape through the airlock.

1. **Pre-Boss Armory & Secret Room 5:**
   - Restock on ammunition in the antechamber.
   - **Secret Room 5 (X: 13, Y: 44):** Press **`[E]`** on the west wall at `(13, 44)` to reveal a concealed pre-boss **200% Supercharge Mega-Health** sphere!
2. **The Colosseum Battlefield (X: 4..58, Y: 49..62):**
   - **8 Obsidian Cover Monoliths:** Heavy stone pillars placed across the arena at coordinates `(12, 52), (24, 52), (36, 52), (48, 52), (12, 58), (24, 58), (36, 58), (48, 58)`.
   - **Low Industrial Barricades:** Center chest-high barricades at `(29, 54)` and `(29, 56)`.
   - **Loop / Shortcut 4:** Outer perimeter trench `(X: 4..10)` allows you to loop completely around charging bosses and attack from behind!
   - **Explosive Barrel Chains:** Barrels positioned around the monoliths allow high-damage explosive chain reactions against demon packs.
3. **Boss Horde:** 2 Arena Cyber-Brutes and a final gatekeeper Brute at `(53.5, 58.5)`. Circle-strafe between pillars, weapon-swap between Super Shotgun and Plasma, and eliminate the threat.
4. **Evacuation Airlock:** Follow the glowing neon green `EXIT ▶▶` markers to coordinate **`(X: 59.5, Y: 59.5)`**. Step into the portal (Tile 6) to achieve victory!

## 3. HOW TO DO IT: CONTROLS & NAVIGATION

| Key / Control | Action | Function & Tactical Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **W / A / S / D** | Move & Strafe | Move forward/backward and strafe horizontally with collision sliding. |

| **M** | Master Audio Mute | Instantly toggles game master audio on or off. |
| **[ / ]** | Volume Controls | `[` reduces volume by 10%; `]` increases volume by 10%. |
| **ESC / P** | Pause & Settings | Opens the in-game pause menu, allowing access to sensitivity, audio, and restart. |

## 4. ARSENAL & WEAPONS GUIDE

### 🗡️ 1. Tactical Combat Knife
* **Hotkeys:** `1` or `Q`
* **Damage:** 38 points per slash
* **Range:** Melee (1.6 units)
* **Ammo Cost:** 0 (Infinite)
* **Best Used For:** Conserving ammunition on solitary low-health Crawlers or finishing off wounded demons.

### 🔫 2. AK-47 Tactical Assault Rifle
* **Hotkeys:** `2` or `Q`
* **Damage:** 25 – 37 points per round (High-velocity hitscan)
* **Rate of Fire:** ~540 RPM (Full-automatic burst)
* **Starting Ammo:** 25 rounds (Max capacity: 150)
* **Best Used For:** Medium to long-range encounters, picking off Imps before they close distance.

### 💥 3. Double-Barrel Super Shotgun (SSG)
* **Hotkeys:** `3` or `Q`
* **Damage:** 14 pellets × (18 – 32) points = **Up to 300+ devastating point-blank damage!**
* **Ammo Cost:** 2 shotgun shells per blast
* **Recoil & Reload:** Heavy kick with authentic double-barrel breech-open reload sequence.
* **Best Used For:** Point-blank annihilation of Cyber-Brutes and high-density demon clusters.

### ⚡ 4. Plasma Carbine
* **Hotkeys:** `3` or `4` or `Q`
* **Damage:** 28 – 40 points per plasma bolt
* **Fire Rate:** High-speed energy projection with bright cyan muzzle flash and minimal recoil.
* **Max Capacity:** 300 plasma cells
* **Best Used For:** Rapid crowd suppression and melting high-health boss demons at range.

## 5. DEMON BESTIARY & THREAT INTEL

### 🕷️ Threat 1: Crawler Demon
* **Health:** 45 HP
* **Speed:** Fast (2.1 units/sec)
* **Attack Style:** Swift quadruped rusher with razor-sharp claw swipes.
* **Damage Dealt:** 25% health per strike.
* **Tactics:** Backpedal while firing the AK-47. One single point-blank shotgun blast or 2 rifle rounds eliminates them.
* **Drop on Death:** Guaranteed Medikit or AK-47 ammo box.

### 👹 Threat 2: Hell Imp
* **Health:** 65 HP
* **Speed:** Medium (1.65 units/sec)
* **Attack Style:** Demonic slasher with lethal melee claws and high aggro range.
* **Damage Dealt:** 25% health per strike.
* **Tactics:** Keep them at medium range. Imp melee attacks are deadly if they trap you against a corner.
* **Drop on Death:** Guaranteed Medikit or AK-47 ammo box.

### 🦹 Threat 3: Cyber-Brute Boss
* **Health:** 200 HP
* **Speed:** Heavy March (1.25 units/sec)
* **Attack Style:** Devastating heavy attack dealing **50% instant damage** (cuts health in half!).
* **Tactics:** 
  1. Never stand still in front of a Brute.
  2. Use the **Shift** key to sprint in wide strafing circles.
  3. Lure them near **green explosive barrels** and detonate the barrels.
  4. Use the Super Shotgun or Plasma Carbine to stagger them.
* **Drop on Death:** **Dual Loot Drop!** Drops both a Medikit/Mega-Health AND high-tier Ammo (Bullets or Plasma Energy Cells).

## 7. COMBAT TACTICS & SURVIVAL TIPS

1. **Always Keep Moving (Circle-Strafing):**  
   Demons calculate intercept vectors toward your last position. By holding **`W + A`** or **`W + D`** while sweeping your mouse, you can circle around enemies while maintaining continuous fire.
2. **Watch the Tactical HUD Banner:**  
   The banner at the top of your screen tells you your exact current objective, the distance in meters to the target, and which direction to walk.
3. **Use the Minimap (`Tab` Key):**  
   When navigating winding corridors or searching for keys, tap `Tab` to display the radar minimap. Walls, doors, keys, and live demon positions are clearly tracked in real-time.
4. **Detonate Explosive Barrels Strategically:**  
   Do not shoot barrels when standing right next to them! Instead, wait until 2 or 3 demons chase you past a barrel, then shoot the barrel to instantly vaporize the entire group without spending heavy ammo.
5. **Prioritize Supercharge Spheres:**  
   Whenever you see a pulsing blue sphere, grab it to reach **200% HP**. This buffer allows you to survive multiple direct hits from Cyber-Brutes.

## 8. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & OFFLINE ENGINE

OFFLINE DOOM is built specifically for zero-network environments:

* **Pure HTML5 Raycasting:** Utilizes classic DDA (Digital Differential Analyzer) raycasting trigonometry running on an unscaled HTML5 2D Canvas context.
* **Procedural Pixel Assets (`js/assets.js`):** Every wall texture, floor tile, demon animation frame, weapon sprite, and projectile is procedurally rendered into memory buffers at boot time.
* **Synthesized Web Audio Engine (`js/audio.js`):** Contains zero external sound files. Every gunshot, shotgun blast, reload clack, door slide, demon roar, pain grunt, and dark atmospheric synthesizer soundtrack is generated in real-time using Web Audio oscillators, biquad filters, white noise generators, and gain envelopes.
* **Local Persistence (`js/storage.js`):** High scores, demon kill counts, best completion times, and user preferences (mouse sensitivity, volume levels, CRT scanlines) are stored in `localStorage` without any cloud dependence.
